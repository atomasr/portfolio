01. Who I am

I'm a designer and creative developer originally from Argentina, now based in Orlando. I like living where design, code and AI meet: building visuals, animations and content that people actually stop for.

I started out as a Full-Stack Developer and Meta Spark AR filter creator, shipping AR experiences for brands like Oreo and Disney+. That grew into 3D art, motion design and video editing, and today my day-to-day is a mix of design, AI-assisted content creation, social media management and animation. Right now, I'm at MediaLab as a Junior Unity Developer, building training simulators.

In my free time, I also build my own games. It's where I get to experiment the most.
